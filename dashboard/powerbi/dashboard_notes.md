# 📊 Dashboard Notes — Supply Chain Analytics Report

This document describes the structure, purpose, and interpretation of the **Power BI dashboard** developed for the Daxwell Data Analyst assessment.

The dashboard is designed as a **two-page interactive report** focused on supply chain performance, order behavior, and revenue insights.

---

# ⭐ PAGE 1 — Executive Overview

## 🎯 Purpose
This page provides a **high-level summary** of operational and supply chain performance, enabling executives to understand:

- Financial performance  
- Order volume  
- Delivery efficiency  
- Inventory risk exposure  

This summary is ideal for **daily or weekly business reviews.**

---

## 📌 KPI Cards (Top Row)

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Total Revenue** | Total sales generated from all processed orders. |
| **Total Orders** | Count of all orders placed within the selected period. |
| **OTIF %** | % of shipments delivered **on time and in full** — a core supply chain KPI. |
| **Avg Delivery Delay (Days)** | Average days deliveries arrive past expected date. |
| **High Risk Inventory %** | % of SKUs classified as **Critical** or **Out-of-Stock**. |

These KPIs provide an immediate snapshot of operational health.

---

## 📈 Daily Revenue Trend (Line Chart)

### **Purpose**
Shows how revenue changes over time, highlighting:

- Demand seasonality  
- Revenue spikes/dips  
- Disruptions or anomalies  

### **How to use**
- Apply filters (date, region, SKU, supplier) to identify performance drivers.  
- Detect sudden demand shifts or operational issues.  

---

## 🥧 Inventory Status Distribution (Pie Chart)

### **Purpose**
Summarizes inventory health across SKUs.  
Categories include:

- Healthy  
- Low  
- Critical  
- Out-of-Stock  

### **How to use**
- Quickly identify inventory risk levels.  
- Support replenishment and procurement planning.  

---

## 🎚️ Slicers (Filters)

| Slicer | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Order Date** | Explore patterns across specific time ranges. |
| **Customer Region** | Compare performance across geographic markets. |
| **SKU** | Drill down into item-level behavior. |
| **Supplier** | Analyze OTIF and shipping reliability by supplier. |

These slicers enhance **interactivity** and enable quick root-cause analysis.

---

# ⭐ PAGE 2 — Orders & Revenue Analytics

## 🎯 Purpose
This page provides deeper insight into customer ordering behavior, revenue distribution, and SKU-level performance.

It is ideal for:

- Diagnostic analysis  
- Planning & forecasting  
- Customer segmentation  
- Operational workload planning  

---

## 📈 Daily Orders Trend (Line Chart)

### **Purpose**
Shows how order volume changes daily.

### **Insights supported**
- Demand surges  
- Capacity planning  
- Order cycle understanding  

---

## 📊 Revenue by Region (Bar Chart)

### **Purpose**
Breaks down total revenue by customer region to identify:

- Strong markets  
- Underperforming zones  
- Sales concentration patterns  

---

## 🥯 Order Priority Mix (Donut Chart)

### **Purpose**
Displays the proportion of:

- **High Priority Orders**  
- **Normal Priority Orders**

### **Why it matters**
High-priority orders may indicate:

- Urgent customer needs  
- Stockouts  
- Fulfillment escalations  
- Short-term demand changes  

---

## 📦 Top 10 SKUs by Revenue (Bar Chart)

### **Purpose**
Identifies the highest revenue-generating SKUs.

### **How to use**
- Understand revenue concentration  
- Support inventory & procurement planning  
- Guide product strategy  
- Evaluate product profitability  

This is one of the **most insightful visuals** for analysts.

---

# 🧭 Dashboard Design Philosophy

The dashboard follows these principles:

### ✔ **Clarity**
Only essential metrics are shown.

### ✔ **Consistency**
KPIs follow standardized definitions across all pages.

### ✔ **Interactivity**
Slicers allow instant drill-down analysis.

### ✔ **Storytelling**
The dashboard flows from:  
**“What is happening?” → “Why is it happening?”**

### ✔ **Scalability**
Can expand into supplier scorecards, logistics dashboards, forecasting pages, etc.
