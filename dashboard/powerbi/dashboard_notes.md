Dashboard Notes — Supply Chain Analytics Report

This document describes the structure, purpose, and interpretation of the Power BI dashboard developed for the Daxwell Data Analyst assessment.

The dashboard is designed as a two-page interactive report focused on supply chain performance, order behavior, and revenue insights.

⭐ PAGE 1 — Executive Overview
Purpose

Provides a high-level summary of operational and supply chain performance, enabling executives to quickly understand:

Financial performance

Order volume

Delivery efficiency

Inventory risk exposure

This page is designed for daily or weekly business reviews.

KPI Cards (Top Row)
KPI	Description
Total Revenue	Total sales from all processed orders.
Total Orders	Count of all orders placed during the selected period.
OTIF %	% of shipments delivered on time and in full — a key supply chain KPI.
Avg Delivery Delay (Days)	Average number of days shipments arrive past their expected date.
High Risk Inventory %	% of SKUs classified as Critical or Out-of-Stock.

These KPIs offer a snapshot of operational health.

Daily Revenue Trend (Line Chart)

Purpose:
Shows how revenue changes over time, highlighting seasonality, spikes, and dips.

How to use:

Filter by date, region, SKU, or supplier to identify performance drivers.

Use it to detect sudden demand changes or disruptions.

Inventory Status Distribution (Pie Chart)

Purpose:
Summarizes inventory health across SKUs.

Categories include:

Healthy

Low

Critical

Out-of-Stock

How to use:
Identify risk levels and evaluate replenishment needs.

Slicers (Filters)
Slicer	Purpose
Order Date	Explore trends across selected time ranges.
Customer Region	Compare performance across geographic markets.
SKU	Drill down into item-level analysis.
Supplier	Analyze OTIF and inventory risk by supplier.

These filters apply cross-report context and drive interactivity.

⭐ PAGE 2 — Orders & Revenue Analytics
Purpose

Provides deeper insight into customer ordering behavior, revenue distribution, and SKU-level performance.

This page is ideal for diagnostics, planning, forecasting inputs, and customer segmentation analysis.

Daily Orders Trend (Line Chart)

Purpose:
Shows how order volume changes daily.

Insights it supports:

Demand spikes

Operational workload planning

Trends across regions or SKUs

Revenue by Region (Bar Chart)

Purpose:
Breaks down total revenue by customer region.

Helps identify:

Strong markets

Underperforming zones

Regional sales concentration

Order Priority Mix (Donut Chart)

Purpose:
Displays the proportion of orders classified as:

High Priority

Normal Priority

High-priority orders may indicate:

Urgent customer needs

Stockouts

Production or fulfillment escalations

Top 10 SKUs by Revenue (Horizontal Bar Chart)

Purpose:
Identifies the most profitable SKUs.

How to use:

Understand revenue concentration

Guide inventory planning

Evaluate product contribution margin

This is one of the most insightful visuals for supply chain analysts.

📌 Dashboard Design Philosophy

The dashboard follows these principles:

Clarity — Displays only metrics that matter for supply chain operations.

Consistency — KPIs use standard definitions across pages.

Interactivity — Slicers allow instant root-cause analysis.

Storytelling — Pages progress from “What is happening?” → “Why is it happening?”

Scalability — Can expand with supplier scorecards or logistics pages easily.

🚀 Business Value Delivered

This dashboard enables Daxwell to:

✔ Identify potential delays and fix bottlenecks
✔ Monitor supplier performance
✔ Track inventory health and risk levels
✔ Understand revenue patterns and customer demand
✔ Support replenishment and planning
✔ Improve OTIF performance
✔ Detect operational inefficiencies
